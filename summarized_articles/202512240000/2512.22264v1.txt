Photonic Neural Networks have shown promise in accelerating machine learning, but current simulation tools face challenges in scalability due to computational demands. To address this, a new method called the Slicing Method has been introduced, which enables more efficient transfer matrix computation. This method has been integrated into a framework called LuxIA, allowing for scalable simulation and training of large Photonic Neural Networks. LuxIA has been evaluated across various architectures and datasets, demonstrating its superiority in speed and scalability compared to existing tools. The Slicing Method reduces memory usage and execution time, making it feasible to explore and optimize larger, more complex architectures. This advancement paves the way for broader adoption of photonic computing in machine learning, enabling more efficient and scalable research and development in the field.
