Summary:

The article presents a new method to accelerate the training of Photonic Neural Networks (PNNs) called the Slicing Method, which is implemented in the LuxIA framework. The Slicing Method reduces memory usage and execution time by efficiently computing transfer matrices for PNNs. This allows for scalable simulation and training of large PNNs.

The article highlights the limitations of current PNN simulation tools, particularly regarding scalability and memory efficiency. It also discusses the challenges of training PNNs, including the need to reconstruct the full mesh transfer matrix M from its constituent layer matrices L i during each backward pass.

The LuxIA framework is introduced as a unified simulation and training framework that supports diverse architectures and datasets. The article presents experimental evaluations across various photonic architectures and standard datasets, demonstrating the effectiveness of LuxIA in surpassing existing tools in speed and scalability.
