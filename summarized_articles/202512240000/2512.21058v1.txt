The UniPath model is a unified large multimodal model that combines pathology understanding and image generation capabilities. It achieves high visual fidelity and realistic tissue textures in generated images, while preserving fine-grained morphological details. The model uses a multi-stream control architecture, which enables fine-grained control over image generation. A large-scale corpus of image-text pairs was curated to support training and evaluation, with a refined subset of 68K high-quality samples. Evaluations demonstrate that UniPath achieves strong image fidelity, robust image-text alignment, and superior concept-level controllability, outperforming state-of-the-art baselines. The model also retains strong diagnostic understanding and is preferred by both machine learning models and human pathologists in pairwise evaluations. Overall, UniPath shows promise for controllable pathology image generation and has the potential to support various downstream tasks.
