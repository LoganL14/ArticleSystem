The proposed framework aims to achieve accurate human motion segmentation by incorporating temporal semantics into the segmentation process. The method utilizes a large language model to identify temporal neighborhoods of each frame, promoting semantic consistency within segments. The approach balances data fidelity, subspace embedding, and temporal smoothness through a joint optimization framework. This enables the model to refine its learned embeddings and achieve improved motion segmentation performance. Theoretical analysis demonstrates the framework's robustness to annotation noise and its ability to maintain temporal coherence. The method's effectiveness is further highlighted through visualizations of embedded features and similarity matrices, showcasing its ability to produce compact clusters and accurate motion segments. Overall, the framework provides a robust and efficient approach to human motion segmentation, leveraging the strengths of both temporal semantics and subspace embedding.
