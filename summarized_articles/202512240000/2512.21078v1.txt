The UniPR-3D framework is a novel approach to place recognition, leveraging 3D tokens to capture stable structural information in scenes. This method demonstrates superior performance in both single-frame and sequence-level matching tasks, outperforming existing baselines. The framework's ability to combine 2D and 3D information enables it to retrieve the correct place even under challenging conditions, such as seasonal, weather, viewpoint, and day-night variations. Experimental results show that UniPR-3D achieves significant improvements over state-of-the-art methods, with gains exceeding 10% in certain scenarios. The approach's strength lies in its capacity to capture spatial information across frames, making it a promising direction for future research in visual place recognition.
