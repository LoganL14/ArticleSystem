The article "Systematic Evaluation of Dynamical Stability in Crystal Generation Models" (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.21227v2) presents a systematic evaluation of dynamical stability in crystal generation models. The authors used eight generative models to generate 221,000 novel structures and performed full phonon-spectrum calculations on 108,843 successfully relaxed crystals.

The key results are:

* The overall stability ratio is 25.83%, with 28,119 structures found to be dynamically stable.
* The top three models are MatterGen (41.0%), InvDesFlow-AL (38.4%), and CrystalFormer (34.4%).
* Models trained on larger datasets exhibit higher performance, while those trained on smaller datasets show lower stability ratios.
* Pre-training on large-scale, high-quality crystal databases can substantially enhance the performance of generative models in predicting crystal dynamical stability.
