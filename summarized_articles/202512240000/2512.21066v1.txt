This study proposes an agentic XAI framework that combines SHAP-based explainability with multimodal LLM-driven iterative refinement to generate progressively enhanced explanations for agricultural recommendations. The framework successfully enhances recommendation quality, peaking at Rounds 3-4, but excessive refinement leads to a substantial drop in quality due to bias-variance trade-off. Strategic early stopping is needed for optimizing practical utility, challenging assumptions about monotonic improvement. The framework's architecture integrates machine learning yield prediction with SHAP-based explanation and multimodal LLM-driven iterative refinement, enabling an agent-like behavior where the MLLM progressively enhances its outputs through self-reflection, supplementary analysis generation, and iterative refinement.
