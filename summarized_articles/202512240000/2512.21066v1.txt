A study was conducted to evaluate the effectiveness of an agentic explainable AI (XAI) framework in enhancing agricultural recommendations. The framework combines SHAP-based explainability with multimodal large language model-driven iterative refinement to generate improved explanations. The results showed that the framework significantly enhanced recommendation quality, with peak performance achieved at rounds 3-4, representing a 30-33% improvement over the initial round. The study used a unique approach, leveraging both human experts and large language models to evaluate the recommendations across multiple metrics. The findings suggest that strategic early stopping is essential for optimizing practical utility, challenging assumptions of monotonic improvement in agentic systems. The study provides evidence-based design principles for trustworthy agentic XAI, including implementing strategic early stopping and integrating hybrid human-LLM evaluation.
